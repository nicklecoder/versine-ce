An idea taught in one skill must reappear wherever it naturally applies, and a skill must present every ordinary shape of its problem (answers of zero, of exactly one, negative, non-whole). A shape met for the first time in an exam is effectively a new topic. Gaps of this kind are found by counting what the catalogue never asks, not by tests.
