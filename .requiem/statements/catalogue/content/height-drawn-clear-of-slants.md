In area and volume figures the height is drawn as an interior dashed line at right angles to the base, with a right-angle mark, and its label sits beside that dashed line clear of every slanted edge; a trapezoid's height drops from the middle of its top, which keeps it well clear of both legs. A height label set on or next to a slant reads as the slant's length, which is the slip the levels exist to correct.
