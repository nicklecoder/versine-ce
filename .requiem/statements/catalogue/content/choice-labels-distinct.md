No two options of a choice question may read the same. In trig-equations General Solutions a + 4πk decoy printed as 2πk, so the right family appeared twice and a guess was a half; a wrong option shown twice wastes a decoy. Options built from sets, families or polynomials can print alike even when their keys differ, so check-answers.py compares the labels themselves, not only the keys the answer is checked by.
