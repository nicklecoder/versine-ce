Measurement contexts use both US customary units (inches, feet, yards, miles; ounces, pounds; cups, pints, quarts, gallons; °F) and metric units (mm, cm, m, km; g, kg; mL, L; °C), not one system alone, and the catalogue teaches converting within each system and between them (a units skill). Students go on to chemistry, physics, geology and astronomy, which work in metric and convert constantly, while daily US life is customary; being fluent in both and in moving between them is setup for those subjects, though they are out of Versine's scope. Cross-system factors are given in the question (1 in = 2.54 cm) rather than expected from memory, except where a level teaches one to remember.
