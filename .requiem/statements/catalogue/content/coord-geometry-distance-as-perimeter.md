coord-geometry practises the distance formula as perimeters of shapes on the grid, not as a level of lone distances, because pythagoras already has a Distance Between Points level and a perimeter needs the formula on every slanted side while adding the run and rise (walking round the corner) stays a visible trap. Partition in a Ratio leaves out 1:1 for the same reason: halfway is the Midpoint level. About 40% of perimeters are built from 3-4-5 style runs and rises so the answer is exact; the rest round to 1 dp.
