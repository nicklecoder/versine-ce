When a level can have no solution, the prompt of every row on it says 'or say there is none', not only the rows whose answer is none, and the solutions box accepts none and no solution as that answer. A prompt that offered it only when it was the answer would give the answer away, and one that never offered it leaves a student who has found nothing unsure whether the box can say so. A level that always has a solution keeps plain 'Solve:' unless its skill words every prompt alike. Applies to rational-eq's Extraneous and rational-functions' Vertical Asymptotes; radical-eq words every prompt this way (radical-eq-asks-for-none), and so do poly-functions' Polynomial & Quadratic Inequalities, in radical-eq's words.
