Every student-facing string (prompts, explanations, blurbs, visual labels, hints) uses US notation: money in dollars and cents ($4.50, 75¢, with US prices that are believable), never £, € or pence; US spelling (center, meter, liter, color, favorite, gray; candy not sweets); a decimal point and comma thousands. Versine's students are in the US and its levels are mapped to Common Core and TEKS, and an unfamiliar currency or spelling is a second thing to decode on top of the maths, which a drill should never ask. Code comments and docs are not student-facing and are left as they are.
