The Sample Spaces level mostly asks learners to list or recognise outcomes rather than count them: which list is the whole sample space, and which outcome is missing from a partial list. Counting by the multiplication principle stays as a smaller share. This follows 7.SP.8b, which asks students to represent sample spaces by lists, tables and tree diagrams; counting alone let a learner skip the listing. Spaces are kept to at most 9 outcomes so a list fits a phone, and decoy lists are truthful (an outcome repeated, missing, or order ignored).
