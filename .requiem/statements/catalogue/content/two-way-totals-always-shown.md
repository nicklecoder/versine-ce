Two-way tables always show their Total column and row, revealed or not, so the table looks the same before and after the answer and a missing total is asked as a '?' cell. The level also includes relative-frequency tables, already in percents of their rows or columns, asking for a missing percent and whether the variables are associated, which S-ID.5 names alongside joint and marginal frequencies.
