Drawing four stacked sigmas in the prompt, lettered A to D, and offering the letters as the choice buttons, so writing a series in sigma could show real stacked limits. Rejected: the letters are a fixed menu, which the medoid gate leaves out, so whether the key sits in the middle of its decoys goes unmeasured; four tall sigmas wrap badly on a phone; and the options sit apart from the buttons a student presses. Spelled one-line labels (Σ from k = 1 to 10 of (2k + 1)) keep the options in the buttons and measurable.
