A probability of at least 1 success, or at most n − 1, is worked as one minus the single case it leaves out, 1 − P(none) or 1 − P(all n), and adding every other case is an aside at most. Binomial Probability once led with adding all six cases of at least 1 rainy day in 6 and gave the complement only in parentheses, which teaches the long way as the way. Where the event leaves out two or more cases (at least n − 2, at most 2) the few cases are added. check-answers.py checks that these rows lead with 1 − q^n or 1 − p^n, the subtraction is right, and it gives the answer.
