rational-eq Work & Rate answers are whole numbers, halves or thirds. A time like 414/5 hours is 82.8, which a student reasonably types, and a fraction box refuses that as a form miss (decimal-in-a-fraction-box), so fifths and quarters are left out rather than taught as a trap. The stories stay believable: a river runs 1 to 6 mph and at most a third of the boat's 10 to 24 mph, since a current at half the boat's speed barely lets it upstream, and a tank with a pipe and a drain fills within a day. No one of the five shapes passes about 40% of the level.
