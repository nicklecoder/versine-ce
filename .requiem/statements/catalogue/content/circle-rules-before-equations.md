The circles angle levels keep x-equations to about a third of the rows, so the level practises the circle rule itself (central versus inscribed, minor and major arcs, opposite angles, angles on one arc) before an algebra layer is wrapped round it. Central & Inscribed Angles and Tangents cap their libraries so the number rows are not crowded out; Semicircles & Cyclic Quadrilaterals reweights its generator instead, adding number questions (two neighboring corners given, an arc from its inscribed angle and back) because its number rows alone were too few to fill a cap. A level that is two-thirds equations tests solving linear equations more than circles.
