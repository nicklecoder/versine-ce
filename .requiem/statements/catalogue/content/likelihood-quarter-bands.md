Describing a probability in words keys "unlikely" only below 1/4 and "likely" only above 3/4, never at the edge, and every "closer to 0 than to 1/2" claim in an explanation must be true of the number shown. Textbooks put the word boundaries in different places, so the bands are stated in the level blurb and only values clearly inside one are asked.
