Graphing inequalities' From Words asks the student to write the inequality a budget or target makes (9x + 4y <= 60, typed and judged as a half-plane), not the largest whole y once x is fixed, because writing the two-variable inequality from the words is what the level's name and blurb promise and the single number never exercised it. The story names what x and y count, since choosing the variables is part of the skill.
