vectors' Unit Vectors asks the unit vector in a direction, a vector of a length along one, and one against one in about 40, 30 and 30 of every hundred rows (they were 22, 53 and 25), and every one says 'Give it exactly': a length along ⟨35, −12⟩ is ⟨945/37, −324/37⟩, a fraction with no ending decimal, and 441 of 1000 rows had such a component without saying so, so a student typing the calculator's <25.54, −8.76> was marked wrong. The vector box asks again for the exact form when a decimal is typed for such a component (play/answers/vector-decimal-asked-exactly), and check-answers.py fails a vector row with a non-ending component whose prompt does not say exactly.
