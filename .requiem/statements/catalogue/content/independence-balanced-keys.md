Independent or Not must key "yes" and "no" about equally, and the size of the shift that breaks independence must come from the numbers rather than a separate random draw, so neither the key nor the look of the numbers gives the answer away. The level also asks whether mutually exclusive events with nonzero probabilities are independent (they never are), since learners often confuse the two ideas.
