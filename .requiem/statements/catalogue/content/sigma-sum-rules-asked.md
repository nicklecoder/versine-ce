Series' Sigma Notation asks the sum rules as well as the reading of Σ: Σc = nc (a constant rule, including from k = 0 or 2, where n is not the top number), Σc·aₖ = cΣaₖ and Σ(aₖ + b) = Σaₖ + nb given only Σaₖ, Σk = n(n + 1)/2 and Σk² = n(n + 1)(2n + 1)/6 at n too large to add by hand, and a polynomial rule split into them, 3Σk² − 2Σk + Σ5. These are the rules Precalculus and calculus use sigma for, and a level that only evaluated short sums term by term never made a student reach for them.
