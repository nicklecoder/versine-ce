Triangle Inequality also asks for the range of the third side given two sides, answered as the strict inequality |a − b| < c < a + b typed into the solutions box, because the range is the standard textbook form of the theorem and writing it as an inequality states both bounds at once. A minority asks for the longest or shortest whole-number third side, where 'as a whole number' makes it a different question (a + b − 1, |a − b| + 1) rather than a stand-in for the range.
