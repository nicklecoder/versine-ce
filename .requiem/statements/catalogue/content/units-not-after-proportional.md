The units skill does not depend on proportional, although proportional has a Converting Units level. A conversion in units is taught as a stated fact and a factor (1 ft = 12 in, so multiply by 12, because a smaller unit takes more of them), which needs nothing of constants of proportionality; making proportional a prerequisite would hold back a pre-algebra skill behind one it does not use. Its edges are decimals, signed fractions for Temperature (a negative times 9/5), unit rate for the rate conversions, and the first levels of area and volume for square and cubic units.
