In unit-circle, sec, csc and cot are taught on their own level (sec, csc, cot, L5), so Signs by Quadrant (L2) asks only about sin, cos and tan and Tangent (L4) only about tan, including where it is undefined. The reciprocals appear from L5 on and in All Together. check-answers.py fails any L2 or L4 row whose text names sec, csc or cot.
