In trig-equations the solving levels (Solve in Degrees, Solve on [0, 2π), General Solutions, Multiple Angles, Quadratic-Type) keep rows that need no reference angle, a value on an axis (0, ±1) or one with no solution, to about a fifth, because the reference angle in the right quadrants is the method these levels teach. The axis values come in more printed forms than the radical ones, so left to chance they filled half of Solve in Degrees.
