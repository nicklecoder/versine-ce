A level of word problems in three unknowns is mostly stories that need all three equations at once, not ones where a relation such as s = 2c or d = n + 3 is put into the others and leaves a one-letter equation after a single substitution: such a story is a word problem in one unknown with extra steps, and a level of them never practices the elimination it is named for. systems-2's Three-Variable Word Problems had about 72% of its rows reduce to one substitution (tickets, coins, three numbers) and one true three-by-three shape (snack-stand orders). Two shapes are added: three items priced two at a time at a ballpark, a school store or a coffee shop (h + s = 7.50, s + p = 8.25, h + p = 10.75, every equation two letters and none given by another, solved by adding all three), and a bakery's batches of muffins, cookies and bread from the flour, sugar and eggs it used (one equation per ingredient, solved by elimination). The relation stories are kept, one in three rows, since naming three amounts and writing a relation is also the skill.
