Every question asking for or giving a direction angle says how it is measured: counterclockwise from the positive x-axis (or from east, in a story), in degrees from 0 to 360. Textbooks also use bearings from north and angles from −180° to 180°, and an answer of −53.1° for 306.9° is the same direction, so a question that left the convention unsaid would mark a right answer wrong. A resultant whose direction rounds near 0° is not asked, since 0.0 and 360.0 would both be fair.
