In a strategy (Which form?) level each situation has one workable choice, or the explanation says honestly that both reach the answer and what the better one saves. Option notes describe what each form looks like (one power, like 3⁴; a plain number, like 81), never what it is for, because a note that says 'so it can be counted or compared' is matched to a prompt that says 'counting' or 'compare' without any judgement. Where size decides (exponents: keep a power when its value is too big to work out by hand), the numbers are chosen so only one choice works, and the two answers are near half and half so always clicking one option does not pass.
