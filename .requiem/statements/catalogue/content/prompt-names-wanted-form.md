A prompt must say what form its answer takes whenever the answer box refuses another correct form: the linear type refuses y - 1 = 2(x - 3) and 2x - y = -1 with a hint to put y on its own, so every 'find the line' question says 'in the form y = mx + b' (or 'as y = ... or x = ...' where a vertical line can be the answer). Otherwise a student who writes a right line in point-slope form, in the very skill that taught it, is bounced for a rule the question never stated.
