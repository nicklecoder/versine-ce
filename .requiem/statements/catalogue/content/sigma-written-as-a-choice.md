Writing a series in sigma notation is asked in series' Sigma Notation as a choice of four sigmas for one written-out series, beside the reverse (which series a drawn sigma is). Choice labels are plain strings (web/ui/choiceinput.js), so a label cannot stack its limits as the sigma prompt term does, and the one-line ∑ₖ₌₁¹⁰ reads its limits as a subscript and a power of the sign; the label spells the limits in words instead, 'Σ from k = 1 to 10 of (2k + 1)', the way the sigma term is read aloud. Σ is still read, never typed. The four options share the rule and cross a slip at each limit: at the bottom, starting a term early (or at 1 for a series from k = 0); at the top, the last term written as the top number, the count of terms written there when k does not start at 1, or the last term's power read as k when the power is k − 1. Letters A-D in the prompt over four drawn sigmas was the other way, turned down because it is a fixed menu the medoid gate cannot measure and it puts the options out of the choice buttons.
