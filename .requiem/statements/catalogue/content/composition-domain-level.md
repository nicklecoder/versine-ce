composition has a Domain of f(g(x)) level before All Together: x must suit g and g(x) must suit f, which neither Domain & Range nor f(g(x)) teaches alone, and a simplified formula hides the inside's limit ((√x)² is x only for x ≥ 0). It depends level-precisely on domain-range's Domain of √ and Fractions, so composition now declares domain-range and opens only after it, as most courses order them. check-answers.py tries f(g(x)) unsimplified at every twelfth, so both limits come from evaluation. Its f(g(2)) level moved from the expression box to the whole-number box, since every answer there is an integer and the expression hint misled.
