Context problems use sizes and speeds the real thing has, in both unit systems: runners 2 to 8 m/s, cyclists up to about 30 ft/s, cars 20 to 75 mph, a 1 : 24 model car 6 to 9 inches (15 to 22 cm), ships, towers and bridges at scales that make them tens or hundreds of meters, rooms 2 to 9 m (6 to 30 ft) each way, walls and garden paths their own size. A 2 ft car, a 14 m car or a 0.5 m room tells the student the numbers are arbitrary and wastes the sense of size the context is there to build.
