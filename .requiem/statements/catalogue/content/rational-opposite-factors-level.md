rational-expr has an Opposite Factors level before All Together: a − x and x − a are opposites, a − x = −(x − a), so they cancel to −1, not 1 -- (16 − x²)/(x² − 11x + 28) = −(x + 4)/(x − 7), (x² − 4)/(2 − x) = −x − 2. Cancelling them to +1 is a mistake common enough to be drilled on its own rather than left to one shape in Simplify.
