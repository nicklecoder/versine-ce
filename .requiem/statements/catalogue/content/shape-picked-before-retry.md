A generator that draws a question shape and then retries until the numbers suit should pick the shape once, before the retry loop, not inside it. Picked inside, a shape whose draws are often rejected (a constant term that exists only for some n) is redrawn as a different shape on every retry, so it ends up far below its stated weight and the shape that rarely fails fills the level: binomial's The Constant Term came out 61% coefficients of other powers until the pick moved out of the loop. Where a shape's whole space is small, a libraryCap on the level keeps a full library from filling up with the larger shape once the small one has run out.
