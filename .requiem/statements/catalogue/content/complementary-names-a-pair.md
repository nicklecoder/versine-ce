The words complementary and supplementary name a pair of angles only. A question with three parts that make a right angle or a straight line says exactly that (the three make a right angle, so they add to 90°) and never calls the three complementary or supplementary, because the textbook definition is two angles and a three-part 'complementary' teaches a meaning the student will be marked wrong for later. Pair questions are the bulk of the Complementary and Supplementary levels; the three-part case is a minority kept for the add-to-the-whole idea.
