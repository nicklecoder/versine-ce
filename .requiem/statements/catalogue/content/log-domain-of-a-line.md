Log Functions asks the domain of the log of a line, log_b(mx + n) + k, and not of x² − c² or c² − x², because x² − c² > 0 is a quadratic inequality, taught in poly-functions' Polynomial & Quadratic Inequalities, which logarithms does not depend on. 65 rows at 85b18ac asked it with nothing behind them; a dependency on poly-functions for one shape would make all of logarithms wait for that skill, so the shape was dropped instead. The domain of a log's inverse, and the range of a log or a power, take its place among the set answers.
