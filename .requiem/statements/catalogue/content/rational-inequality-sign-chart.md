Rational inequalities are a level of poly-functions, Rational Inequalities after Polynomial & Quadratic Inequalities, not of rational-functions. The method is the sign chart the level before teaches, with the bottom's zeros added as places the sign can change and never included, so it is met while that chart is fresh; and rational-functions already has eight levels with Intercepts and Match the Graph, so a ninth would need a longerBecause. The cost is one gate: poly-functions now depends on rational-expr (Excluded Values), whose own prerequisites are all already below poly-functions. Rows mix one factor over one, three critical numbers, a squared top factor that does not change sign, rows with a number on the right ((6x − 21)/(x − 5) ≥ 3) whose explanation says not to multiply through by x − 5 but to take the number over first, and k/(x² + m) rows true everywhere or nowhere, so every prompt offers none. check-answers.py reads each half-step from −60 to 60 by putting it into both sides as written, an x that makes a denominator 0 being out.
