An answer kept in terms of π has a coefficient that is whole or ends within two decimal places (12.5π, 6.25π), never 15.625π, and is written π, not 1π. Long coefficients test long multiplication rather than the arc or sector rule, and a student cannot enter a fraction in the π box to avoid them.
