Every numeric answer on a level goes in the same kind of answer box, so the box never tells the student what kind of number the answer is. In unit-circle and identities L1 a rational value (4/3) and an irrational one (√7/4) were keyed as frac and roots, and the frac box against the roots box gave the answer's form away before any working; now every number on those levels is a roots answer, which accepts plain fractions and decimals like 2.5 exactly (4/3, −4/3, 8/6 all parse). A choice question is a different kind of question and keeps its options.
