vectors gains a Dot Product level before Resultants: u · v = ac + bd, the angle between two vectors from cos θ = u · v / (|u||v|) to 1 decimal place, Orthogonal / Parallel / Neither (a fixed menu, the answer drawn first so each is the key a third of the time), and the k that makes two vectors orthogonal. The dot product and the angle between vectors are in every precalculus course and are used in calculus and physics (work, projections), so a student finishing the catalogue without them is not ready for calculus (principles/ready-for-calculus). It makes vectors nine levels, declared in longerBecause.
