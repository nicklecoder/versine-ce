In trig-graphs' Sinusoidal Models, a Ferris wheel's height equation h = c − r cos(2πt/T) is printed only on questions that ask for the height at a time t. Printed on a question asking for the amplitude, midline, period or b, the equation is the answer written out, and the question tests reading coefficients instead of modeling the wheel.
