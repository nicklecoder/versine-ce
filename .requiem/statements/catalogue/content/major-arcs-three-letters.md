An arc of 180 degrees or more is named with three letters, arc ACB, with the third point C drawn and lettered on the arc; two-letter names (arc AB) are for minor arcs only, as US geometry courses write them. 'Arc AB measures 250 degrees' contradicts the convention every textbook and test uses. Sectors bounded by a major arc are named by their radii and that arc.
