The probability skills accept a fractional probability in any equivalent form (requireSimplest is false), so 6/36 is marked right for 1/6. The count over the count is the probability skill's lesson, and simplifying is the fractions skills' lesson. A student who correctly counts 6 of 36 should not be marked wrong for stopping there. The stored answer and the explanation still give the lowest terms. The skill also opens with a Likelihood level that the Phase 3 plan did not list, for 7.SP.5's 0-to-1 scale. It asks only about probabilities at 0, 1/2 or 1, or at least 0.2 away from 1/2, so "likely" and "unlikely" are never a judgment call.
