The circles skill gives semicircles, angles on the same arc and cyclic quadrilaterals a level of their own (Semicircles & Cyclic Quadrilaterals), beside Central & Inscribed Angles, though the Phase 3 plan listed only the latter. They are the inscribed angle theorem's consequences that G-C.3 names, and each has its own trap: halving 180° for the semicircle, adding to 180° rather than setting equal for angles on one arc, and setting opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral equal as in a parallelogram. Folded into one level they crowded out the theorem itself.
