In the series skill an infinite geometric series is typed only when it has a sum, and whether one has a sum is asked as a choice of four series, half the rows asking which one has a sum and half which one has none. The fraction box cannot say 'no sum', and offering none on every typed row would need a box that reads it; the choice row makes the ratio test the question itself, with ratios of 1 and −1 among the series with none, so terms that merely do not grow are not mistaken for a sum. A typed row that always has a sum gives nothing away, since the level's choice rows are where the test is asked (catalogue/content/none-offered-on-every-row covers levels whose typed rows can be empty).
