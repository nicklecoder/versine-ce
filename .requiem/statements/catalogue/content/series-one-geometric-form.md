Series works every finite geometric sum in the one form the skill teaches, Sₙ = a₁(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r), even where r > 1 makes both brackets negative (2,000(1 − 1.1³)/(1 − 1.1) = 2,000(−0.331)/(−0.1)): Series in Context's growth stories and Working Backward's geometric counts once switched to a₁(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1), which is the same formula but reads to a student as a second one to learn. Division tops that are fractions are bracketed, (5/3)/(1 − 1/3), and a positive ratio after a minus is not, since 5/3/(1 − (1/3)) reads as two divisions.
