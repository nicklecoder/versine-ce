radians' Reference Angles mostly asks angles in one turn past quadrant I, and in about a quarter of rows a negative angle or one past a full turn, in degrees or radians, so the student finds the terminal side first (−150° is coterminal with 210°). The figure draws the turn as given, clockwise for a negative angle and as a widening spiral past 360°. check-answers.py brings the angle into one turn by stepping, then measures to the x-axis.
