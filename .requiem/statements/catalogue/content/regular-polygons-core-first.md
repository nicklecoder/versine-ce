Regular Polygons is mostly the core questions — interior or exterior angle from the number of sides, sides from an exterior or interior angle or from the interior sum, interior from exterior and back — because those are what the level teaches and what a test asks. The polygons-meeting-at-a-point puzzle is a minority (under a tenth), always drawn as a figure, and names a repeated shape once with a count (two regular octagons and a square), never 'a regular decagon and a regular decagon'.
