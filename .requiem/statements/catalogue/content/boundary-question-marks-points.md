A question about how a boundary is drawn (dashed or solid, which side) must not draw a neutral copy of the line while asking, because the plane draws answer marks on top of question marks and a neutral solid line shows through the gaps of the revealed dashed one, so a dashed edge looks solid on the very levels that teach dashed versus solid. Mark two whole-number points on the line instead and let the reveal draw the line through them. A renderer-side ask-only mark would let the neutral line come back.
