Sigma notation is drawn by a sigma prompt term, Σ with its limits stacked above and below it and the rule after, and in the series skill it is only ever read: a sum written in it is evaluated, its terms counted, or matched to the series it writes out, and the answer is a number or a choice. Written on one line as ∑ₖ₌₁¹⁰ the limits read as a subscript and a power of the sign, and a stacked sign is how every textbook sets it. Writing a sum in sigma notation is asked by choosing which written-out series a sigma is, not by typing one, because Σ has no key and a sigma typed into an expression box would need a parser for limits that nothing else uses; the choice still tests reading both limits, since its wrong options start a term early and stop a term early.
