In Reflect, Rotate and Dilate the cases about the origin or over the axes are the majority, because those are what the coordinate rules ((x, y) → (−x, y), (−y, x), (kx, ky)) describe and what a course assesses first. Reflection over a line such as x = k or y = k is at most a third of Reflect Over an Axis, and rotation or dilation about another center is a small minority of its level.
