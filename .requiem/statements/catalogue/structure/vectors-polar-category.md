Vectors and parametric/polar share a new category, Vectors & Polar (vectors-polar), under the Trigonometry subject, rather than joining Coordinates or Functions. Both are worked with cos θ and sin θ throughout -- a vector's components and direction angle, a polar point's x = r cos θ and y = r sin θ, a parametric curve traced by an angle -- so they open after the unit circle and sit beside it on the map. Coordinates already holds eight skills, and filing them there would put two trigonometry-heavy skills among skills a student meets before any trigonometry.
