Every explanation must be true for the exact numbers and picture of its own row, not for the typical row its template was written for. A template that holds for most rows and is false for some is the defect to look for: Even or Odd said 'f(2) = 1 and f(−2) = 1, neither the same nor opposites' because ±2 are roots of x³ − 4x, so the test point is chosen to show the verdict; Name the Parent described every parabola as 'a U, curved at the bottom' beside one that opens down, so the wording follows the sign. The same goes for grammar that depends on a number ('1 second', '160 − x', not '1 seconds', '160 − 1x') and for steps that are only there when the row has them ('take away the number in front' when there is none).
