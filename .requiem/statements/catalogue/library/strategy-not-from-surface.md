A choice level's right option must not be readable from a surface feature of the prompt that is not the reason for the choice. Quadratic Formula's Which Method? had every non-monic equation answered 'formula' and every monic one with an odd middle 'factor', so 'a > 1 means the formula' scored 100% while teaching a false rule (2x² + 7x + 3 factors). Each answer's pool therefore spans the surface features the other answers have -- non-monic equations that factor, monic odd-middle ones that do not -- and a row whose question presupposes something false (a method for an equation with no real roots, the way to the zeros of a parabola that has none) is left out rather than given a misleading answer.
