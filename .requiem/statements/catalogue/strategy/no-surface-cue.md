A strategy level must not be answerable from a surface cue. No word in the prompt (exact, roughly, add, ruler) and no fixed template may settle which option is right on its own: each answer occurs under shared wording, at least some situations go either way depending on their numbers, and across the library the right answers are near even. A level of eight templates with one answer each was learnt in a sitting and then passed without reading the numbers (decimals Which Form?, factors GCF or LCM?, rounding Exact or Estimate?, review October 2026), which drills recognition of a template rather than the choice between procedures the level exists for.
