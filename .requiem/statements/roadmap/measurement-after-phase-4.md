The measurement groundwork for experiments (recording which variant of a screen each student saw, and delayed retention re-tests) starts after Phase 4 of the catalogue plan is built, not alongside it. The owner chose to finish coverage first so the builder and review cycle are not split across two tracks; the experiment phase then begins with the groundwork as its first step.
