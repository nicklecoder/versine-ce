Open question: how far past precalculus should the catalogue go -- Calculus I (limits, derivative rules, applications), Calculus II (integration techniques, series), or stop at precalculus? Calculus is very drillable (each rule is a procedure with a checkable answer), which fits a drill system, but a derivative or integral has many correct spellings, so it needs an answer type that judges by value, which play/answers/expr-canonical-order-only deliberately rules out for expressions today. What hangs on it: whether a calculus answer type is designed, and whether the experimentation phase starts after precalculus or after calculus.
