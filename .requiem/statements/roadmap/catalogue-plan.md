The skills still to build are planned in docs/catalogue-plan.md: about 67 new skills in five phases (finish pre-algebra, functions and linear, polynomials/quadratics/exponentials, geometry with chance and data, trigonometry and precalculus), each listing its levels and the standards it covers. The list was derived by checking every Common Core content standard for grades 6-8 and high school, plus Texas TEKS Grade 8 through Precalculus, because there is no federal curriculum and those two frameworks bound what states require. Covering them is the floor for 'ready for calculus', not the ceiling. The plan keeps the catalogue rules: about eight levels per skill, split by depth, at most ten skills per category, and edges only where genuinely needed, so geometry, data and trig never gate the algebra spine and the road to Algebra is not lengthened.
