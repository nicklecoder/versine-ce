New skill coverage continues through precalculus (the end of Phase 4 in docs/catalogue-plan.md); then the work shifts from adding skills to experimenting on how Versine teaches: how problems are presented and described, how instructions are delivered, and how the UI is laid out, each tried in variants with real students and kept only where it measurably helps learning for students who use it regularly. Versine is a system for practising and drilling, not a complete curriculum, so coverage stops where more breadth would add less than getting the practice itself right.
