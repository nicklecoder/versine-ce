A plane whose axes measure something named in the story carries titles {x, y} (linear-models A Graph's Story: hours, km from home), drawn at the far end of each axis in the muted colour, because a story graph read without them is two bare number lines and the student has to carry the units from the prompt in their head. Plain coordinate questions leave them off: x and y are the titles there.
