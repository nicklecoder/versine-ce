Committed answer syntax, not to be revised casually: ^ for exponents (** also accepted), sqrt()/√, root(n, x), implicit multiplication, pi, e, abs(), round brackets only. ^ because Desmos, WolframAlpha, TI-84 and LaTeX use it; square brackets and braces are reserved for intervals and sets.
