A decimal typed into the fraction boxes is read for its value: right, it is refused with 'That is the right value as a decimal. Write it as a fraction instead.'; wrong, it is plainly wrong. The numerator box used to strip the point, so 0.25 became 25 and was marked wrong as a different number with no hint -- across every fraction answer, including probability, where 0.25 is a natural thing to type. The level's answer is a fraction, so the right decimal is a form miss, not a pass.
