The vector box reads the unit-vector form in any order and spelling a student reaches for: 3i − 4j, −4j + 3i, 3i + −4j (plus a negative), 3i + (−4)j and (3/5)i + (4/5)j (a bracketed coefficient), i − j, 2j. Each term is its signs, a coefficient bare or in brackets, and i or j, each letter at most once and a missing one 0; anything else is still refused with the hint to use angle brackets. Refusing a right vector because its terms were written j first or with a bracket asks the student to guess the box's grammar.
