A line or rule with a fractional slope is formatted 3x/2 (and −x/2), never 3/2x: written on one line, 3/2x reads as 3 over 2x, and the form shown on reveal and stored as the first accepted form must not be ambiguous. Typed answers are still judged by the line, so 1/2x typed by a student is accepted as the parser reads it.
