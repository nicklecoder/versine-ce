Where factors are drawn side by side, as typed without a times sign, a bracket typed around a product or a negative is kept, and a number following something is always bracketed. Without them (−2a³)(3b⁶) draws as −2a³b⁴3b⁶ and 2(3) as 23, which read as one term or one number. The bracket is remembered on the parsed tree for drawing only; canonical comparison ignores it, so judging is unchanged.
