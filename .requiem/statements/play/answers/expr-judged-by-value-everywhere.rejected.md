Loosening the expr type to judge every expression answer by value. Rejected: on levels where the arithmetic or the form is the point (Collect like terms, Expand, Factor, vertex form) 5 for 2 + 3 or x² + 6x + 9 for (x + 3)² must stay refused. Value judging is an opt-in type a level chooses instead.
