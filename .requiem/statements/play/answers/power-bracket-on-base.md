A power of a number set after another factor keeps its bracket on the base, not round the whole power: 500(1.04)^x is drawn 500(1.04)ˣ and 2x(3)^2 as 2x(3)², because that is how exponential models are written in every textbook and how the student typed it. Bracketing the whole power, 500(1.04ˣ), was correct but read as a different expression from the one on the page.
