An answer is right when it equals the stored value or any other form the problem lists in accept. Canonical comparison sees order but never arithmetic, so it cannot tell that (x + 3)² is (x + 3)(x + 3); a level where two finished forms are both right lists the second rather than the comparison being loosened for every level.
