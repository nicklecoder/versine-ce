Making a right-but-unfinished answer (or any form refusal) cost no attempt, so a student refused for writing x/4 − 2/4 or the wrong form is not penalised. Rejected: a form check is part of what a level asks, and play/answers/rounding-keeps-places already says it costs the attempt. The unfairness it was meant to fix -- correct, finished answers refused because the box compared by form -- is fixed by judging such levels by value (play/answers/equivalent-judged-by-value), not by making refusals free.
