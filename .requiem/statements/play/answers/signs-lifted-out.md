Before an expression answer is canonicalised for order, its sign is lifted out of products, double negations and quotients, so −2x + 3 matches 3 − 2x and −4/x², −(4/x²) and 4/(−x²) are one answer. Where the minus is written is not a form a level can ask for: moving it is not arithmetic, and refusing it marked right answers wrong — first for a leading minus in a sum, then for a negative power finished below the bar in Monomials.
