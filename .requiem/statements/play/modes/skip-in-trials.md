Open question: should a timed run (Time Trial) let a stuck student skip a problem? Today every problem must be answered: 'always another go, the cost is time'. One problem a student cannot do can drain the whole clock, and there is no walkthrough in a trial. What hangs on it: a skip changes what clearing a trial proves (target solved vs target attempted), how a skip is logged for the teacher view, and whether skipping is free or costs seconds. Practice already nudges towards the walkthrough after three misses.
