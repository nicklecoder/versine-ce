Any comparison between students, such as a leaderboard or a race, is scoped to one household or one class and never shared across them, so it stays among people who already know each other. Ranking teenagers against strangers invites exactly the discouragement and cheating a small known group does not, and it keeps server/client-scores-with-cap adequate: a self-scored result is only ever seen by people the student knows.
