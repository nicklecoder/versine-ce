Restructuring a skill's worth as a base from its cleared levels' accuracy plus a bounded speed bonus earned from how fast the student clears the last level's Time Trial, shown on cards as 'Level +7.4 · speed bonus +1.2 of 3.0'. Considered and set aside: today's per-level quality already is a base (accuracy, 60% of weight) plus a bounded speed share (40%, full at 0.6x the fixed reference pace), and moving speed into the finish would shift every student's Level once for a gain not yet shown to be needed. Worth reopening if students treat speed on earlier levels as irrelevant, or the finish needs its own pull.
