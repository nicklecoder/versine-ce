Students must be pulled both to revisit finished skills and to move forward. Prompts tied only to the Level panel reached the student who cares about Level and not the one who finishes and moves on, so review state lives on the skill cards and at the start of the session, where every student meets it. Whether ignoring a due review should also cost points is review/turn-on-decay; the user explicitly accepts overriding earlier no-decay rules if the data shows it improves motivation.
